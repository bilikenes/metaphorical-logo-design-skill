# Animal and Object Pairing

## Reference examples

Use these pairings to understand how the animal can carry meaning while the object gives that meaning a physical form. The readings below describe the imagery and can guide new briefs.

| App | Pairing | Metaphoric reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demet | Monkey and bouquet | Dexterous hands gather and hold the stems together, making assembly legible as a single bouquet. |
| Blazon | Chameleon and folder | The chameleon's ability to change appearance represents changing the folder's appearance. Its grasp and curling tail visually connect the two subjects. |
| Zihin | Squirrel and sheet of paper | The squirrel's habit of gathering and saving gives the sheet of paper the role of an idea worth keeping. |

For a new product, derive new pairs from the brief. The animal, object, pose, and tile color in a supplied reference belong to that reference. Carry them into a new design only when the brief requests them.

## Choosing the three concepts

Start with the app's core verb and the physical thing that best conveys it. Explore different ways to make the interaction visible. An animal may gather, grip, wrap, carry, protect, or reshape the object where its anatomy and behavior plausibly support that action. Seek different outlines as well as different metaphors. A beak lifting something into the air makes a different composition from a paw holding it close or a body coiling around it.

Record a short title and one sentence for each idea that connects the app's benefit, the animal's trait, and the object's role. Favor images that answer both questions at a glance: what is the animal doing, and why is that the object it is doing it to?

## Image prompt template

> Generate one original engraving as a high-resolution square PNG with a true transparent background. Render the pair entirely in black ink and ivory, using natural-history copperplate engraving, fine directional hatching and crosshatching, and anatomically credible forms. Use the supplied image as a reference for the engraving and tonal treatment. Preserve ivory highlights as visible paint. Leave the space around the pair and through any gaps fully transparent.
>
> The app helps people {CORE_ACTION_OR_BENEFIT}. The only two foreground subjects are one {ANIMAL_SPECIES} and one {IDENTIFIABLE_PHYSICAL_OBJECT}. The animal's {RELEVANT_TRAIT_OR_BEHAVIOR} expresses {METAPHOR}. Show it {VISIBLE_ACTION_WITH_OBJECT}. Set the scene in {VIEWPOINT_AND_POSE}, with {CONTACT_AND_OVERLAP} joining the two forms. Keep the object's identifying features visible and keep both forms legible in a single silhouette at app-icon sizes.
>
> Center the combined silhouette with breathing room on all sides for a rounded-square tile to be added later. Keep detail subordinate to the large shapes. Exclude a drawn tile, checkerboard pattern, color field, additional creatures, loose items, scenery, lettering, interface elements, gradients, shine, cast shadows, and cartoon outlines.
